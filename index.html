About the author

J.R.H.

J.R.H. writes science fiction about ordinary people confronting extraordinary events. His stories explore family, survival, courage and the choices humanity makes when the future is uncertain.

His debut novel, Continuity: A Road to the Rift, begins a planned trilogy set on a frozen Earth. He is also developing The Rubin Anomaly, a cosmic-horror science-fiction series about a discovery that should never have been made.

“Limitations are the ones you set yourself.”

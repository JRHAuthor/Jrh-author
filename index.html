Behind the stories

About J.R.H.

J.R.H. is a British science-fiction author whose stories explore survival, family, moral choice and humanity's place in the universe.

Born with four learning disabilities, he spent years believing that writing and publishing a novel was beyond him. Becoming a father changed that. Inspired by his daughter, his fiancée and his family, he chose to prove that limitations do not have to define what a person can achieve.

His debut novel, Continuity: A Road to the Rift, is the opening chapter in an expanding collection of ambitious, connected science-fiction stories.

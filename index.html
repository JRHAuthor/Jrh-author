About the author

J.R.H.

Hi everyone, and welcome to my debut novel, Continuity: A Road to the Rift (Book One).

Continuity is the first book in a planned trilogy, and I hope you'll enjoy following the journey as the series unfolds.

Everything in this book has been edited by me. I'm not a professional author with a large budget for editors—I'm simply an ordinary person with a story to tell who refused to let that stop me.

For most of my life, I believed writing and publishing a novel was beyond my reach. I was born with four learning disabilities, and for years I lacked the confidence to even try. That changed when my beautiful daughter was born.

At just three days old, she suffered a neonatal stroke. She now lives with epilepsy and cerebral palsy, and it is unlikely she will ever walk. Yet every single day she inspires me with her strength, determination and courage. More than anything, I want her to grow up believing the words that have become our family's motto:

"Limitations are the ones you set yourself."

If this book proves anything, I hope it proves that dreams are worth chasing, no matter the obstacles.

None of this would have been possible without the love and support of my wonderful fiancée and my amazing stepson, who inspire me every day.

I'm excited to continue this journey with you. Continuity: Beyond the Rift (Book Two) is planned for release later this year, and the first novel in a completely separate science-fiction series, The Rubin Anomaly, will follow afterwards.

Thank you for picking up my book and visiting my website. I truly hope you enjoy reading Continuity as much as I enjoyed writing it.

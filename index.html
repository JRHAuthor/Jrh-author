About the author

Big ideas. Human stories.

Hi, I’m J.R.H., a science-fiction author from Wales and the creator of the Continuity series.

I’ve been fascinated by science fiction for as long as I can remember. Whether it’s humanity’s survival, first contact, distant worlds or the mysteries of the universe, I’ve always been drawn to big ideas that make you wonder, “What if?”

One of my biggest inspirations is A.G. Riddle, whose novels encouraged me to finally begin writing stories of my own. I’m also a huge admirer of Isaac Asimov, whose work helped shape my love of classic science fiction and ambitious world-building, and Stephen Baxter, whose scientifically grounded stories have inspired my fascination with humanity’s place in the universe.

Outside writing, I’m an enthusiastic Pokémon card collector. Like science fiction, it’s a hobby that has stayed with me over the years and continues to bring me a great deal of enjoyment.

Writing my first novel was something I once believed was beyond my reach. I was born with four learning disabilities, and for many years I lacked the confidence to pursue my dream of becoming an author. Eventually, I decided the only way to know what was possible was to try.

None of this would have been possible without the love and support of my wonderful fiancée, my amazing stepson and especially my beautiful daughter. At three days old, she suffered a neonatal stroke and now lives with epilepsy and cerebral palsy. Her strength, determination and courage inspire me every day.

“Limitations are the ones you set yourself.”

That journey led to the publication of my debut novel, Continuity: A Road to the Rift. The complete Continuity trilogy, The Last Fleet: When the Flags Fell and The Rubin Anomaly: The Distortion are now available.

Thank you for visiting and supporting my work. I hope you enjoy exploring these worlds as much as I’ve enjoyed creating them.

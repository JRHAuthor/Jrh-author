About the Author

J.R.H.

Hi, I'm J.R.H., a science-fiction author from Wales and the creator of the Continuity series.

I've been fascinated by science fiction for as long as I can remember. Whether it's stories about humanity's survival, first contact, distant worlds or the mysteries of the universe, I've always been drawn to big ideas that make you wonder, "What if?"

One of my biggest inspirations is A.G. Riddle, whose novels encouraged me to finally begin writing stories of my own. I'm also a huge admirer of Isaac Asimov, whose work helped shape my love of classic science fiction and ambitious world-building.

Outside of writing, I'm an enthusiastic Pokémon card collector and enjoy building my collection whenever I get the chance. Like science fiction, it's a hobby that has stayed with me over the years and continues to bring me a lot of enjoyment.

Writing my first novel was something I once believed was beyond my reach. I was born with four learning disabilities, and for many years I lacked the confidence to pursue my dream of becoming an author. Eventually, I decided that the only way to know what was possible was to try.

None of this would have been possible without the love and support of my wonderful fiancée, my amazing stepson, and especially my beautiful daughter. At just three days old, she suffered a neonatal stroke and now lives with epilepsy and cerebral palsy. Despite the challenges she faces, she inspires me every single day with her strength, determination and courage. She reminds me that our family's motto is:

"Limitations are the ones you set yourself."

That journey led to the publication of my debut novel, Continuity: A Road to the Rift. Its sequel, Continuity: Beyond the Rift, is now available, with Continuity: Legacy After the Rift coming soon. I'm also developing new science-fiction projects including The Rubin Anomaly and The Last Fleet.

Thank you for visiting my website and supporting my work. I hope you enjoy exploring these worlds as much as I've enjoyed creating them.
